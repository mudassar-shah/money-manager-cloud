App Lock

Ask for a PIN before the app opens. This stops someone casually picking up your phone and reading your finances — it is not full security, because anyone technical could get past it, and your data also lives in your Google Sheet. Your real protection is your Google sign-in and your device's own lock.

This device is using the built-in code that guards the public link. Set your own PIN below to replace it — once you do, the built-in code stops working here and only your PIN opens the app on this device.

App Lock is ON. It asks for your PIN when the app opens, and again after 10 minutes away.

Your recovery code — write it down, it won't be shown again:
—

If you forget your PIN: open your Google Sheet, go to the Settings tab, delete the row whose first cell says pinHash, then reload the app. It will open unlocked and you can set a new PIN. Only you can do this, because only your Google account can open that Sheet — so you can never be locked out of your own data.

Activity Log

A plain record of everything that changed — what, when, and which device it was done on. Useful for answering "when did this entry change, and what was it before?"

The device name stays on this device only — it is never synced, so your phone and your computer each keep their own name. Everything else in the log is shared between your devices.

WhenDeviceActionDetails

This is a record for you, not tamper-proof proof. It is stored alongside your data, so anyone who could change your data could change this too. Only the newest 400 entries are kept.
